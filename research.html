Research

Our research program is organized into three integrated thrusts.

Thrust 1: Machine-Learned Interatomic Potentials with Long-Range Physics

Automated active-learning workflows for MLIP training and robust long-range interactions.

Thrust 2: AI-Guided Discovery of Safer, High-Performance Biomedical Monomers

Computational-experimental co-design with UB School of Dental Medicine.

Thrust 3: MLIP-Driven Characterization of Real-World Experiments

Pairing MLIPs with synchrotron experiments at Argonne National Laboratory.
