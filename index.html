Our rubric

Algorithmic cores for real-world experimental practice.

Experimental agreement is a test, not an assumption. We compare simulated observables with measured data to identify useful predictions and model limitations, then use those findings to guide model refinement and application-specific studies.

1Develop

Build scalable MLIP workflows and corrections for structures where locality alone is insufficient.

2Ground

Compare simulations with beamline measurements from disordered oxides and layered chalcogenides.

3Translate

Work with UB School of Dental Medicine collaborators to explore candidate materials and their atomistic mechanisms.
